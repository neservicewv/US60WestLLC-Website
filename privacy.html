Privacy Policy

Personal information may include:

  • Full name
  • Mailing address
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Driver's license or government-issued ID
  • Emergency contact information
  • Payment information
  • Rental history
  • Communication preferences
  • Any additional information you provide

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Website usage statistics
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process rental inquiries
  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Verify customer identity
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate regarding your rental
  • Send invoices and payment reminders
  • Respond to questions or requests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Detect fraud or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our business, customers, and property

By providing your contact information, you consent to receive communications from US 60 West Storage LLC regarding:

  • Billing reminders

6. How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or alteration.

While we strive to maintain secure systems, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Information Sharing

US 60 West Storage LLC does not sell your personal information.

We may share information only when necessary with:

  • Payment processors

  • Collection agencies or attorneys when necessary to enforce a Rental Agreement or recover unpaid amounts

Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to operate our business or comply with applicable law.

We retain customer information only for as long as necessary to:

Our website and services are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that such information has been collected without parental consent where required, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:
