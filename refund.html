Refund Policy
US 60 West Storage LLC
Effective Date: August 2, 2026

At US 60 West Storage LLC, we strive to provide clear and fair billing practices. This Refund Policy explains how payments, cancellations, and refunds are handled for our self-storage services.

By renting a storage unit, making an online payment, or using our services, you agree to this Refund Policy.

1. Monthly Rental Payments

All storage rentals are provided on a month-to-month basis.

Monthly rent is due in advance according to your Rental Agreement. Payments are applied to the current rental period and are considered earned once the rental period begins.

2. No Refunds for Early Move-Out

US 60 West Storage LLC does not provide prorated refunds or credits when a customer vacates a storage unit before the end of the current rental period.

Once a rental period has begun, the full monthly rent remains due regardless of the move-out date.

3. Online Payments

Payments made through our website are generally considered final. Please review all payment information carefully before submitting payment.

If you believe a payment was made in error, contact us immediately so we can review the transaction.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you believe you have been:

  • Charged twice,
  • Charged the incorrect amount, or
  • Experienced a payment processing error,

please notify us as soon as possible. Verified billing errors will be corrected promptly, and any approved refund will be issued using the original payment method whenever possible.

5. Administrative Fees

Administrative fees, processing fees, and similar non-rental charges are generally non-refundable unless required by law or determined by US 60 West Storage LLC to have been charged in error.

6. Security Deposits

If a refundable security deposit is required under your Rental Agreement, it will be returned only after:

  • Your account is paid in full;
  • The storage unit has been completely emptied;
  • The unit has been inspected by management;
  • No damage beyond normal wear is present;
  • All contractual obligations have been satisfied.

Any unpaid rent, fees, damages, cleaning charges, or other amounts owed may be deducted from the deposit as permitted by the Rental Agreement or applicable law.

7. Reservation Payments

If reservation fees or prepaid move-in payments are offered, any applicable refund eligibility will be disclosed at the time the reservation is made.

Unless otherwise stated, reservation payments are non-refundable after a storage unit has been assigned or held for the customer.

8. Abandoned Property

No refunds or rental credits will be issued for units deemed abandoned or for accounts that become subject to lien enforcement or other remedies available under the Rental Agreement or Kentucky law.

9. Exceptions

US 60 West Storage LLC reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to issue a full or partial refund in extraordinary circumstances, including:

  • Billing errors;
  • Duplicate payments;
  • Technical payment processing issues; or
  • Other situations where management determines a refund is appropriate.

The issuance of one refund does not create an obligation to provide refunds under similar circumstances in the future.

10. Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days after approval. Actual processing times may vary depending on your financial institution or payment provider.

11. Chargebacks

Customers are encouraged to contact US 60 West Storage LLC before initiating a chargeback with their bank or credit card issuer.

If a chargeback is filed for a valid charge, we reserve the right to provide supporting documentation to the payment processor and pursue any remedies available under the Rental Agreement or applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

US 60 West Storage LLC reserves the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes become effective immediately upon posting to our website.
